# 🏙️ Sky City Tower

A city-skyscraper building game inspired by tower sims like Happy Tower. Build floors upward, open businesses, house residents, hire them into their dream jobs, stock products, and ride shoppers up the elevator.

## Play

Open `index.html` in any modern browser — no build step or dependencies. Progress saves in your browser's local storage.

To play online, enable **GitHub Pages** (Settings → Pages → Deploy from branch → `main` / root).

## How it works

- **Build floors** – Choose one of 8 business categories or a Living Space. Each floor costs more than the last, and the exact business is revealed when construction finishes.
- **Business categories** – Food & Beverage, Entertainment & Leisure, Healthcare & Wellness, Technology & Software, Retail & E-Commerce, Education & Training, Finance & Professional Services, Construction & Trades (6 businesses each, 3 products each).
- **Living Spaces** – Apartments, Hotel, Motel, Resort, Villa. Each holds 5 residents; the type sets their skill range.
- **Employees** – One employee per product (3 per business). A product can only be stocked once someone is hired.
- **Dream jobs** – Every resident dreams of a specific business at skill ★1–★5. At their dream job they stock cheaper and faster: 10% / 15% / 25% / 35% / 50%.
- **Elevator** – Tap RIDE to deliver shoppers; each buys 1 item and tips. Only VIPs buy in bulk.
- **VIP shoppers** – Send them to a floor of your choice:
  - 👷 Builder Boss – finishes construction
  - 🤑 Big Spender – buys out a whole floor
  - 🛍️ Collector – buys out a single product
  - ⚡ Speedy Stocker – finishes stocking
  - 📦 Supply Tycoon – fully stocks a floor
- **Gems** 💎 – Earned from new floors, fully staffed businesses and lucky tips; used to rush timers.
